Research & Publication

Submitted • Paper ID 36

Plant Disease Detection Using a Custom CNN
on the PlantVillage Dataset

Submitted to the 5th International Conference on Emerging Trends in Computing Technologies (ICECT 2027), NUML Islamabad — IEEE Xplore proceedings. Sole author. The paper includes Grad-CAM evidence and the PlantDoc field test (8.90% on 236 field photos), which shows exactly where lab-trained models break in the real world — and why that matters for farmers.
